About the Systematic Review

Soft-tissue and bone tumours (STBT) are rare and diagnostically challenging lesions. This systematic review provides an overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI) methods in radiological imaging for diagnosing and prognosing these tumours. It evaluates study adherence to the CLAIM and FUTURE-AI guidelines, aiming to enhance trustworthy and clinically translatable AI development.

Key Findings

  • 325 articles reviewed from 15,015 abstracts.
  • Moderate adherence to CLAIM (28.9/53) and low adherence to FUTURE-AI (5.1/30).
  • AI tools are at a proof-of-concept stage with significant room for improvement.

Implications

  • Define unmet clinical needs and intended settings for AI integration.
  • Focus on training data that reflect real-world scenarios and explainability.
  • Address biases and ensure reproducibility with public data and code.
